## Working hours

Office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Sunday is the weekly off.
A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed. Arriving after 9:15 AM is marked late.
Three late marks in a month count as one half-day deduction.

## Leave

Every permanent employee gets 12 casual leaves and 7 sick leaves per calendar year.
Casual leave must be applied for at least one day in advance through the HR department.
Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate.
Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December. Unused sick leave can be carried forward up to 21 days.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per the Maternity Benefit Act.

## Holidays

The company observes 10 paid public holidays a year. The holiday list is published by HR in January.

## Salary and payroll

Salary is credited to the employee's bank account on the 7th of every month.
Payable salary = monthly gross salary / calendar days in the month x paid days.
Paid days include present days, weekly offs, paid leave and holidays. Absent days and leave without pay are deducted.

## Overtime

Work beyond 30 minutes after shift end counts as overtime, in steps of 15 minutes.
Overtime is paid at twice the ordinary hourly rate, as per the Factories Act.

## Resignation and notice period

The notice period is 30 days for staff and 60 days for managers.
Salary for the last month is settled within 45 days of the last working day.
